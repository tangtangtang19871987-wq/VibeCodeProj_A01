## Scope — read this before the tables

The paper's **"Ours" rows cannot be reproduced in this environment** and are
not claimed to be. They require pretraining on LithoBench (unreachable here,
G-041) using 8x A100 GPUs (this host is CPU-only, G-040).

What follows compares **our independent numerical-ILT baseline**, on physics
verified bit-exact against the public contest checker, with the paper's
published columns. Our solver is a reimplementation: the paper never
specifies its solver, citing CurvyILT (ISPD'25) by reference only (G-015).
A gap between our baseline and the paper's numbers is therefore expected and
is **not** evidence for or against the paper's claims.

> **Partial run:** 3/10 ICCAD13 cases complete (1, 2, 3). Remaining cases pending.

### PV Band (nm²), same runs

**Read this column with care.** Our ILT objective uses `weight_pvb = 0`,
i.e. it optimises nominal-corner L2 only and does **not** target the process
window at all. The paper's solver lineage (CurvyILT) does. Our PV Band being
worse is therefore an expected consequence of a documented configuration
choice (G-015), not a defect in the physics -- which is bit-exact against the
reference checker. A PV-aware variant is a one-line config change
(`weight_pvb > 0`) and is left as a declared, un-run ablation rather than
tuned until the numbers flatter the comparison.
